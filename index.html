LevelProof Gaming

LevelProof Gaming is a creator-operated workflow for preparing and publishing short-form gaming videos to the LevelProof YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube Data API to identify the authorized channel and to upload videos after authorization by the channel owner.

How it works

The application requests YouTube upload and read-only channel access from its channel owner. It prepares vertical gaming edits from footage the creator is authorized to use, and can privately test uploads before public publication. It is not a public signup or third-party video hosting service.

Only the authorized channel owner may connect their Google account. The app does not sell Google user data.
